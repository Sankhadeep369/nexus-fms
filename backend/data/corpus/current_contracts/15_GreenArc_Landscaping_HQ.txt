# 1. Parties

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northbridge Meridian Holdings LLC (the “Client”) and GreenArc Landscape & Groundskeeping LLP (the “Vendor”).

The Client owns or operates the facility known as Northbridge Meridian Tower located at 4100 Trinity Plaza, Dallas, TX 75201, USA.

# 2. Purpose

The Vendor shall provide landscape services for the Client facility. Scope summary: Maintenance of lawns, planters, exterior hardscape, and irrigation for the corporate campus.

# 3. Site Scope

The service area covers: Front lawn, entry planters, patio seating areas, perimeter hedges, and irrigation controllers.

# 4. Scope of Services

| Item | Service / Task | Frequency / SLA | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lawn mowing and edging | Weekly | Maintain grass height, borders, and debris removal. |
| 2 | Shrub and planter care | Bi-weekly | Prune, fertilize, and replace dead plants as required. |
| 3 | Irrigation checks | Monthly | Inspect sprinklers, controllers, and water coverage. |
| 4 | Seasonal beautification | Quarterly | Refresh flower beds and seasonal décor where approved. |

# 5. Commercial Terms

| Charge Item | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly maintenance fee | $2,800 | Includes standard landscaping labor. |
| Plants and materials | At cost | Pre-approved purchases only. |
| Ad hoc enhancement work | $120 per hour | For special event preparation. |

Invoices shall be submitted monthly unless otherwise stated. The Client may reasonably withhold payment for disputed items until resolved.

# 6. Vendor Obligations

• Provide trained personnel, tools, and consumables necessary to perform the services in a safe and professional manner.

• Comply with all applicable laws, site rules, and permit-to-work requirements.

• Notify the Client promptly of any hazard, defect, or condition that may affect safety, service continuity, or compliance.

• Maintain service records, inspection logs, and corrective action reports for the term of the Agreement.

# 7. Client Obligations

• Provide reasonable site access, work permits where required, and a designated point of contact.

• Review and approve any chargeable parts, replacements, or out-of-scope work before procurement or execution.

• Ensure the Vendor is informed of any site-specific hazards or operational restrictions.

# 8. Safety, Compliance, and Insurance

The Vendor shall perform the services in accordance with accepted industry practice and shall implement appropriate safety controls, lockout/tagout procedures where applicable, and personal protective equipment requirements.

The Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable general liability and workers’ compensation coverage and shall provide evidence upon request.

# 9. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall remain in effect for twelve (12) months from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.

Either party may terminate for material breach upon written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure, except where immediate termination is justified by safety, fraud, or gross misconduct.

# 10. Confidentiality and Records

The Vendor shall keep confidential all non-public information obtained in connection with the services and shall use such information only for performance of this Agreement.

Service reports, checklists, and incident records relating to the facility shall be retained for at least three (3) years, unless otherwise required by law or site policy.

# 11. Miscellaneous

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the services described herein and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

The governing law shall be the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
