# 1. Purpose

Ensures the facility can obtain, maintain, and demonstrate compliance with fire safety requirements while keeping response readiness high.

# 2. Scope

Covers fire NOC files, hydrants, extinguishers, alarms, sprinklers, smoke detection, fire doors, evacuation plans, drills, and inspection schedules.

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Fire inspection register, drill attendance sheet, equipment service certificates, NOC file, incident record, corrective action tracker

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Keep fire exits unlocked as required; never obstruct sprinklers or detectors; isolate temporary fire risks with permits and fire watch; replace discharged or damaged extinguishers immediately; learn the evacuation script and assembly point roles; treat repeated false alarms as a corrective-action issue.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Alarm activation or visible smoke/fire | Raise alarm, evacuate, and call response | Fire Officer + Security + Site Head |
| Major | Disabled fire equipment or expired certificate | Escalate and replace within deadline | Facilities Manager |
| Regulatory | NOC deviation or authority notice | Close corrective actions and document evidence | Compliance Owner |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
