# 1. Purpose

Standardizes how guards, supervisors, and facility staff protect people, assets, and evidence through consistent patrol, monitoring, and reporting.

# 2. Scope

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

CCTV uptime log, incident register, visitor log, access card issue log, patrol log, evidence handover form

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Never leave gaps in evidence chain; preserve original footage; report unusual loitering, forced entry, or seal tamper immediately; restrict access to control rooms; use calm verbal escalation before physical intervention; protect personal data and follow privacy rules.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Minor access discrepancy or lost visitor pass | Correct and record | Security Supervisor |
| High | Intrusion, theft, violence, or sabotage | Secure scene and call emergency response | Site Head + Police if required |
| Critical | Evidence compromise or major breach | Preserve logs and escalate instantly | Compliance + Legal + Management |
