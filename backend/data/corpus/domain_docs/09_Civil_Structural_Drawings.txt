# 1. Purpose

Protects the building fabric through inspection, controlled repair, and clear drawing-based recordkeeping for civil assets.

# 2. Scope

Includes masonry, plaster, flooring, ceilings, waterproofing, ramps, roads, kerbs, handrails, doors, finishes, and structural observation points.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Civil Supervisor | Inspects defects, plans repairs, and records closeout. |
| Facilities Manager | Approves scope, shutdowns, and contractor engagement. |
| Structural Consultant | Reviews major cracks, settlement, or load concerns. |
| Contractor | Executes repairs as per approved method statement. |
| HSE Officer | Confirms work area barriers and fall protection. |

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Condition survey report, crack monitoring log, waterproofing history, repair order, drawing revision register

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Do not ignore small cracks that change in pattern or width; protect occupants from falling debris; use proper curing and barricading; record exact location and dimensions; obtain engineer review before altering load-bearing elements.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Finish damage, minor crack, or local dampness | Repair and monitor | Civil Supervisor |
| High | Structural concern or falling material | Restrict access and call engineer | Facilities Manager + Consultant |
| Critical | Collapse risk or major safety hazard | Evacuate and isolate area | Site Head + Emergency team |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
