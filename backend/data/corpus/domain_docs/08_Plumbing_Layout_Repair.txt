# 1. Purpose

Standardizes how plumbing assets are inspected, repaired, and documented to reduce leaks, blockages, water loss, and service disruption.

# 2. Scope

Applies to toilets, wash basins, urinals, traps, floor drains, valves, pumps, water closets, pipe networks, and drainage lines.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Plumber | Performs inspections, unclogging, fixture repairs, and leak correction. |
| Facilities Engineer | Approves major repairs and monitors repeat failures. |
| Housekeeping Team | Reports overflow, blockage, and abnormal odor quickly. |
| AMC Vendor | Handles specialized materials and major replacement work. |
| HSE Officer | Ensures hygiene and confined-space precautions. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Inspect exposed piping, joints, valves, and supports | No dripping or looseness | Inspection sheet |
| Weekly | Flush and clean floor drains / gully traps where required | Drainage remains free-flowing | Cleaning record |
| Weekly | Review repeat complaints and root cause | Trend is understood and addressed | Repeat defect tracker |
| Monthly | Audit pipe route drawings and valve tags | Isolation points are identifiable | Valve map register |
| Monthly | Test pumps and drainage devices where installed | Backup flow works | Functional test log |
| Monthly | Plan replacement of worn fixtures and seals | Predictive maintenance is in place | Replacement plan |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Repair ticket log, valve schedule, blockage report, water leak register, cleaning checklist, drawing revision log

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Confirm water isolation before repair; do not use makeshift joints for permanent fixes; clean up standing water immediately; protect finished surfaces during breaking work; validate drainage after every repair; maintain hygiene controls in washroom areas.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Single fixture defect | Repair same day or next service window | Plumber |
| Medium | Repeated blockage or concealed leak | Investigate root cause and monitor | Supervisor |
| High | Major flood or sewage backflow | Isolate area and call emergency response | Facilities Manager + HSE |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
