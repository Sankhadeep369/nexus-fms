# 1. Purpose

Keeps HVAC equipment performing within temperature, humidity, filtration, airflow, and energy targets while ensuring preventive maintenance and AMC obligations are tracked.

# 2. Scope

Covers chillers, AHUs, FCUs, cooling towers, pumps, condensers, filters, thermostats, dampers, BMS points, and associated controls.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| HVAC Technician | Performs inspections, cleaning, belt/filter checks, leakage detection, and operational tests. |
| AMC Vendor | Delivers contract maintenance, spares, consumables, and service reports. |
| Facilities Engineer | Reviews logs, validates performance, and approves shutdowns. |
| BMS Operator | Monitors alarms, temperatures, and schedules. |
| HSE Representative | Ensures lockout/tagout and work-at-height controls. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Check supply/return air temperatures, differential pressure, alarms, and water levels | Parameters remain within target band | HVAC daily log |
| Daily | Inspect visible leaks, unusual noise, vibration, and filter condition | No abnormal condition left unattended | Patrol log |
| Daily | Review BMS alarms and trend deviations | Any out-of-range condition is assigned immediately | Alarm register |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Clean strainers, inspect belts, tighten fasteners, check drain pans | Equipment remains clean, secure, and leak-free | Weekly maintenance sheet |
| Weekly | Verify thermostat calibration spot checks and sensor readings | Occupied spaces maintain comfort setpoint | Calibration check log |
| Weekly | Review AMC pending actions and spare parts status | All open items have ETA and owner | AMC tracker |
| Monthly | Inspect chillers/AHUs/FCUs as per OEM schedule | Preventive tasks completed and signed | PM report |
| Monthly | Test safeties, interlocks, and emergency shutdown logic | Controls trip as designed | Functional test record |
| Monthly | Review energy consumption versus tonnage / occupancy | No unexplained increase | Efficiency dashboard |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Daily log, weekly PM sheet, AMC service report, breakdown report, spare consumption record, calibration certificates

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Always isolate electrical supply before opening panels; use PPE for refrigerant and rotating parts; never bypass safeties; verify condensation drainage to avoid slips; keep chemical cleaning controlled; maintain access clearance around plant.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Loss of cooling in occupied area | Move to backup mode / portable support and notify | Facilities Manager + AMC Lead |
| Major | Repeated alarm or compressor trip | Stop equipment and diagnose root cause | Site Head + OEM / AMC |
| Emergency | Safety hazard, refrigerant leak, or electrical smell | Isolate area, evacuate if required | HSE + Emergency response |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
