# 1. Purpose

Maintains potable, domestic, and utility water quality while keeping treatment equipment and piping assets serviceable.

# 2. Scope

Includes pumps, tanks, softeners, filtration, chlorination, sampling, pipe routes, valves, pressure control, and water quality records.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Water Technician | Checks treatment plant operation, sample points, and leakage. |
| Facilities Engineer | Reviews test results, failures, and corrective actions. |
| AMC Vendor | Services treatment equipment and dosing systems. |
| HSE Officer | Ensures chemical handling and confined space controls. |
| Housekeeping Lead | Keeps tank rooms, sumps, and sample points clean. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Check tank levels, pump status, pressure, and visible leaks | Supply stable and leak-free | Water log |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Flush dead legs and low-use points as per plan | Stagnation risk reduced | Flushing log |
| Weekly | Check pipe supports, joints, and corrosion signs | No visible deterioration | Inspection report |
| Weekly | Review water meter readings and abnormal consumption | Leaks or wastage identified | Consumption log |
| Monthly | Send water samples for quality testing | Results meet standards / internal limits | Lab report |
| Monthly | Service filters, softeners, and dosing units | Equipment remains effective | PM report |
| Monthly | Review tanks, cleaning schedule, and disinfection plan | No overdue cleaning | Tank maintenance record |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Water quality test register, tank cleaning log, chemical issue log, leak report, meter reading sheet

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Use approved chemicals only; label dosing containers; never enter tanks or pits without permit and gas testing where applicable; protect potable water from cross-connection; isolate contaminated lines and flush after repairs; keep sample chain-of-custody intact.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Minor leak or pressure fluctuation | Repair and monitor | Supervisor |
| High | Water contamination or disinfection failure | Stop use and isolate affected line | Facilities Manager + HSE |
| Emergency | Tank, pump, or pipeline failure affecting operations | Activate contingency supply | Site Head + Utilities |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
