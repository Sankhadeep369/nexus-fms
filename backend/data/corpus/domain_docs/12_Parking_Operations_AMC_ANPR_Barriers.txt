Keeps vehicle movement safe and predictable through defined parking rules, system checks, and maintenance routines.

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Keep pedestrian crossings visible; never block fire lanes; train staff on manual barrier operation; protect ANPR privacy and system credentials; record vehicle damage or access disputes immediately; coordinate with fire and emergency plans.

| Medium | ANPR mismatch or repeated congestion | Escalate to vendor and adjust flow | Facilities Manager |
| High | Accident or emergency access blockage | Clear lane and activate response | Security + Emergency Lead |
