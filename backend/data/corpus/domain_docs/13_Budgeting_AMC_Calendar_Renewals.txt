# 1. Purpose

Provides a disciplined annual budgeting and renewal process tied to current service contracts and operational commitments.

# 2. Scope

Covers AMC planning, contract renewals, forecast cycles, budget approvals, current vendor baseline, procurement lead time, and cost review.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Budget Controller | Maintains annual plan, accruals, and renewal calendar. |
| Facilities Manager | Confirms technical scope and service continuity needs. |
| Procurement / Contracts | Manages RFQ, renewals, and commercial terms. |
| Finance | Approves budget, accruals, and variance tracking. |
| Current Vendor Owner | Confirms current service baseline and scope assumptions. |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Annual budget, AMC calendar, renewal log, current vendor baseline sheet, PO tracker, variance report, approval matrix

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Maintain a current-vendor baseline for every service before comparing alternatives; separate mandatory safety spend from discretionary spend; track contract dates early; do not allow service continuity gaps; document assumptions behind each budget line.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Budget variance above threshold | Investigate drivers and reforecast | Budget Controller |
| High | Critical renewal due within 30 days | Escalate for approval and continuity | Finance + Facilities |
| Critical | Service loss due to missed renewal | Invoke contingency service arrangement | Site Head + Procurement |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
