# 1. Purpose

Transforms utility consumption into a managed process with metering discipline, audit actions, and operational targets.

# 2. Scope

Covers meters, submeters, demand monitoring, energy audits, load scheduling, conservation initiatives, and reporting against targets.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Energy Manager | Sets targets, reviews trends, and initiates improvements. |
| BMS / Metering Operator | Maintains meter accuracy and data capture. |
| Facilities Manager | Implements operational changes affecting consumption. |
| Vendor / Auditor | Conducts formal audit and recommends actions. |
| Finance Representative | Tracks savings, budget, and payback impacts. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Capture main meter and critical submeter data | Readings are complete and consistent | Meter log |
| Daily | Monitor peak demand and unusual load spikes | Excess load identified early | Demand dashboard |
| Daily | Check switched-off areas, lights, and HVAC schedules | No obvious waste remains | Walk-through log |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Compare consumption per occupied area / operating hour | Trend is improving or explained | Weekly energy report |
| Weekly | Verify meter communication and data gaps | No missing data | Meter health report |
| Weekly | Review candidate energy saving actions | Actions are prioritized | Action tracker |
| Monthly | Review audit findings and action closure | Savings actions are tracked to closure | Audit action register |
| Monthly | Assess target versus actual consumption | Variance is explained | Target dashboard |
| Monthly | Publish management summary with opportunities | Leadership gets clear next steps | Energy KPI pack |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Meter register, audit report, utility invoice tracker, target sheet, energy-saving action log, savings validation sheet

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Never optimize energy at the cost of life safety or comfort-critical operations; verify metering accuracy before analyzing data; keep baselines stable enough to compare; document assumptions; coordinate with operations before schedule changes.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Single sensor or data gap | Correct and backfill if possible | Energy operator |
| Medium | Unexpected monthly overrun | Investigate load drivers and propose controls | Energy Manager |
| High | Sustained high demand or utility risk | Escalate to leadership for action | Site Head + Finance |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
