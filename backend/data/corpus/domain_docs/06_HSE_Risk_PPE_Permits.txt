# 1. Purpose

Creates a practical HSE operating layer for all work on site, especially maintenance, contractor activity, and non-routine operations.

# 2. Scope

Covers risk registers, PPE issue and use, work permits, toolbox talks, incident reporting, near-miss management, and contractor safety oversight.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| HSE Officer | Defines control measures, audits work, and closes safety actions. |
| Line Manager | Ensures staff comply with permits, PPE, and training. |
| Permit Issuer | Reviews task risk and authorizes controlled work. |
| Contractor Supervisor | Briefs workers and maintains discipline on site. |
| Worker / Technician | Uses PPE correctly and reports hazards immediately. |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Risk register, incident report, permit log, PPE issuance register, toolbox talk record, training matrix

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Do not start work without permit where required; PPE is the last line, not the primary control; stop work if conditions change; use barricades and signage for open hazards; document every near miss; revalidate risk before non-routine jobs.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | PPE non-compliance or housekeeping issue | Correct immediately | Supervisor |
| Major | Unsafe act, near miss, or permit deviation | Stop work and investigate | HSE Officer |
| Critical | Serious injury or life-threatening hazard | Emergency response and scene control | Site Head + HSE + Medical |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.

# 10. Permit and PPE Control Matrix

| Activity | Mandatory Control |
| --- | --- |
| Hot work | Permit, fire watch, extinguishers, and isolation |
| Work at height | Harness, anchor check, barricade, and rescue plan |
| Electrical work | LOTO, tester check, and competence |
| Confined space | Entry permit, gas test, attendant, and rescue |
| Chemical handling | Gloves, goggles, MSDS, and spill control |
