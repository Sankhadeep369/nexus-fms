# 1. Purpose

Maintains a clean, safe, and organized facility environment while controlling pest risk and space clutter.

# 2. Scope

Covers cleaning schedules, consumables, hygiene checks, pest inspections, storage rules, shared areas, and move management.

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Cleaning schedule, pest log, consumables register, occupancy map, move request form, quality audit checklist

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Separate food waste and standing water from work areas; do not mix chemicals without guidance; keep chemical stores locked; maintain ventilation when cleaning; tag abandoned items before disposal; monitor recurring pest sources rather than only treating symptoms.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Minor cleanliness issue | Correct during shift | Housekeeping Lead |
| Medium | Recurring pest or clutter issue | Escalate and trend analysis | Facilities Coordinator |
| High | Infestation or hygiene-related complaint | Immediate action and vendor callout | Site Head + HSE |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
