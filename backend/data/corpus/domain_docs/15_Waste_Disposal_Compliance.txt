# 1. Purpose

Ensures waste is segregated, stored, moved, and disposed of in line with internal standards and external compliance expectations.

# 2. Scope

Covers waste bins, labeling, storage areas, pickup schedules, manifests, compliance records, and safe handling of general and industrial waste.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Waste Coordinator | Monitors segregation, storage, and disposal records. |
| Housekeeping Team | Collects and labels waste correctly. |
| Vendor / Recycler | Picks up authorized waste streams and provides proof. |
| HSE / Compliance | Checks legal and site compliance. |
| Area Owner | Prevents mixing, dumping, or overflow in own area. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Verify segregation at source for wet, dry, recyclables, and industrial waste | No mixing at bins | Waste inspection log |
| Daily | Remove full bins and confirm labeled collection | No overflow or odor | Collection sheet |
| Daily | Check waste rooms for spill, pests, and safe storage | Waste area remains controlled | Waste room log |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Review pickup schedule and manifest completeness | Every pickup is traceable | Manifest register |
| Weekly | Inspect hazardous or industrial waste storage areas | Storage is secure and compliant | Storage audit |
| Weekly | Confirm bins, liners, and signage are in place | Users understand segregation | Bin audit |
| Monthly | Audit waste volume by stream and disposal route | Management sees waste trend | Monthly waste report |
| Monthly | Review vendor licenses, permissions, and expiry dates | Only approved vendors operate | Compliance file |
| Monthly | Run spot checks on records and training | Process knowledge is maintained | Training log |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Waste manifest, vendor authorization file, bin inspection sheet, disposal receipt, spill log, complaint log

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Never leave waste uncovered or mixed; keep industrial waste classified and labeled; use PPE for sharps or contaminated items; record chain-of-custody for pickups; clean spills immediately; keep storage areas away from food and potable water lines.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Segregation issue or overflowing bin | Correct and retrain | Waste Coordinator |
| High | Illegal dumping, spill, or missing manifest | Escalate and isolate area | HSE + Compliance |
| Critical | Hazardous exposure or regulatory breach | Immediate containment and notification | Site Head + Legal/Compliance |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
