# 1. Purpose

Ensures every vendor is screened, contracted, inducted, monitored, and offboarded with a clear record trail.

# 2. Scope

Applies to all facilities, security, safety, and support vendors, including AMC providers, contractors, consultants, and service agencies.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Contracts Manager | Owns contracts, renewals, scopes, and signatures. |
| Facilities Owner | Defines the technical scope and acceptance criteria. |
| Procurement | Checks commercial terms and documentation. |
| HSE / Compliance | Reviews safety, insurance, and legal compliance. |
| Vendor Manager | Coordinates onboarding, access, and performance tracking. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Track active vendor attendance, work permits, and site entry | Only authorized personnel work on site | Entry / permit log |
| Daily | Verify open service requests and completion evidence | Work is closed with evidence | Service ticket log |
| Daily | Capture deviations from contract scope or SLA | Exceptions are documented | Exception register |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Review vendor performance, quality, and response times | Issues are visible early | Scorecard |
| Weekly | Confirm insurance, license, and compliance documents are current | No lapse in mandatory docs | Compliance tracker |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Contract register, onboarding checklist, insurance copies, license register, SLA scorecard, scope acceptance form, offboarding checklist

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Do not allow work to begin before induction, insurance, and authorization are complete; keep evidence for every service claim; align scope, price, and acceptance criteria; record deviations immediately; tie performance review to measurable service outcomes.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Missing document or minor SLA drift | Correct and follow up | Vendor Manager |
| High | Unauthorized work, unsafe act, or contract breach | Stop work and escalate | Contracts Manager + HSE |
| Critical | Fraud, major non-compliance, or site risk | Suspend vendor and initiate review | Site Head + Legal/Procurement |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
