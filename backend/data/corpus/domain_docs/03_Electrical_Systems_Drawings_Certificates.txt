# 1. Purpose

Controls the safe operation and documentation of electrical distribution, backup power, and statutory certificates across the facility.

# 2. Scope

Includes transformer rooms, LT panels, DBs, DG sets, UPS, batteries, cable routes, lighting, earthing, protection devices, and single-line diagrams.

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Panel logbook, SLD register, test certificates, relay calibration reports, shutdown permit, incident log

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Do not open live panels without authorization; maintain LOTO and discharge checks; keep drawings updated after every change; maintain dry floors and cable trenches; use insulated tools and arc-flash precautions; only certified persons may perform energization or termination work.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Single lamp, socket, or minor DB issue | Repair during routine window | Supervisor |
| Medium | Repeated tripping or unknown load increase | Investigate and isolate affected circuit | Facilities Manager |
| High | Fire/smoke, shock risk, or main incomer trip | Emergency isolation and evacuation | Site Head + HSE + Utility provider |
