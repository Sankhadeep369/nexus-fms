# 1. Purpose

Prepares the site to continue critical functions and respond effectively to incidents through tested continuity plans and drills.

# 2. Scope

Covers emergency plans, recovery priorities, communication trees, evacuation, sheltering, business continuity, and after-action evaluation.

# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency Controller | Leads incident command and recovery coordination. |
| Facilities Manager | Protects critical utilities and facility continuity. |
| Security Lead | Manages alarms, access, and evacuation control. |
| IT / BCP Lead | Supports data, systems, and alternate work modes. |
| Department Heads | Confirm critical staff, processes, and recovery needs. |

# 4. Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

| Frequency | Task | What Good Looks Like | Evidence / Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Check readiness of emergency contacts, radios, and key systems | Communication tools are functional | Readiness log |
| Daily | Verify emergency exits, signage, and kits | No critical item missing | Daily emergency round |
| Daily | Monitor for conditions that could trigger emergency mode | Early warning is escalated | Watchlist log |

# 5. Preventive Maintenance and Periodic Tasks

| Frequency | Task | Control Point | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Review emergency supplies, batteries, torches, and first aid | Kits are stocked | Inventory log |
| Weekly | Test notification tree or mass message process | Messages reach responders | Communication test |
| Weekly | Validate critical utility fallback arrangements | Fallback can be executed | Continuity checklist |
| Monthly | Run scenario-based drill or tabletop exercise | Roles are understood and tested | Exercise report |
| Monthly | Evaluate response times and decision points | Lessons are identified | After-action report |
| Monthly | Update continuity procedures and recovery dependencies | BCP remains current | BCP revision log |

# 6. Records, Logs, and Registers

Emergency contact tree, drill report, BCP register, lessons learned log, critical asset list, alternate site plan

# 7. Precautions and Critical Controls

Practice the sequence before crisis day; protect life first, then critical operations; document decisions during incidents; keep fallback copies of plans off-site; include vendors in recovery dependencies; convert lessons learned into a tracked action list.

# 8. Escalation and Incident Response

| Severity | Trigger | Action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Localized emergency with limited impact | Execute response and monitor | Emergency Controller |
| Critical | Life safety event or major utility failure | Activate command center and evacuation | Site Head + All responders |
| Recovery | Post-incident service restoration | Track actions to stable closure | BCP Lead |

# 9. Training Notes for SLM Use

For training and model fine-tuning, keep each action paired with the required record, the triggering condition, and the expected outcome. Prefer concise operational verbs such as inspect, isolate, record, notify, verify, and close.
